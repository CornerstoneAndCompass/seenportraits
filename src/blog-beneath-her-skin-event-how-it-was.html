Beneath Her Skin Event: A Photo Chronicle

Exhibition Opening Night & Charity Event

The Photo Chronicle

Our series “Beneath Her Skin” culminated with an exhibition at our Melbourne studio in September 2023. I was honored to reveal the light and depth of each woman, celebrating our talents, beauty and creativity through my photography. Some participants shared their experiences, inspiring others to see the real women behind the project and demonstrating that this transformative experience is attainable for everyone, regardless of age, shape, or belief system.

Our guests left feeling profoundly empowered! We raised over $5,000 for Rotary Peninsula 2.0 in our charity auction and donated to women and children in Ukraine affected by the war.

My heart is full, and I’m deeply grateful to everyone who joined us, for the generous donations, and for the unparalleled atmosphere we created together!

Enjoy the photo story!

Much love,

Marina
