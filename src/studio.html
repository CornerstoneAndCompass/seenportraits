The space

Designed for style and creative versatility

SEEN Studio is designed with an emphasis on style and creative versatility for various portrait photography styles. You and your loved ones will feel comfortable and relaxed, like at home.

Our comfortable studio is designed to help you fully immerse yourself in the moment with your loved ones. No wind or sand on your feet, let’s capture these fleeting and invaluable moments in a truly memorable atmosphere.

Feel at home in our warm ambient space where each client is given comfort and personalized attention by our all-female team. We take an intimate approach, carefully considering styling, posing and lighting to ensure that you look and feel your absolute best.

Our Melbourne studio is based on inclusivity, ensuring everyone has a safe space to feel like themselves and look their best.

02

On the day

The studio is reserved exclusively for you

Hair and makeup

You will be attended by our expert hair and makeup artists. Sitting in the makeup chair can help you relax and ease any nerves, while the makeup itself helps the camera capture the best possible lighting on your face.

The shoot

The atmosphere is set with fresh flowers, scented candles, and your favourite music. You are guided through elegant poses in your natural flow, so your photos are authentic and connected.

The screening room

After a break, you join us in our screening room for a private photo presentation, where you relax and choose your favourites, and we show you the finest ways to turn them into art.
