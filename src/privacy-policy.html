WHAT INFORMATION IS COLLECTED?

We collect several types of information in order to be able to provide and improve our service to you through the Website.

Internet Traffic Data

Internet traffic data includes but is not limited to internet service provider information, collection of IP addresses, entry and exit pages, various operating systems and other such information. We collect no personally identifying information through this process, and it is used to help analyze user traffic with respect to various services available through SEEN Portraits to allow for a more customized and enjoyable user experience.

Cookies Data

We use “cookies” on this Web Site. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous identifier, usage of cookies is in no way linked to any personally identifiable information on our Website.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some options on the Website.

Personal Data

While using our Website, we may ask you to provide us with basic personal data that can be used to contact or identify you. Personal data may include, but is not limited to:

If you would like us to remove your testimonial or customer review at any time, please contact us and we will promptly do so.

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that information from a person under the age of 18 was collected via the Website without parental consent or verification, we will take steps to remove that information from our server/database.

In order to use some options on this Website, you may need to complete the registration form. During registration you are required to give certain information (such as name and your contact details). This information is used to contact you about the products/services on our Website in which you have expressed interest.

Participation in these surveys or contests is completely voluntary and you may choose whether or not to participate and therefore whether or not to disclose information sought by such surveys or contests.
