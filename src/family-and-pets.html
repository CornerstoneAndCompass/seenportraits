Family FAQ

We provide exceptional service from start to finish, and if you have any questions that aren’t answered below, please don’t hesitate to ask

Where are you based?

SEEN Portraits family photography studio is based in Cheltenham, Victoria. This is located in the South-East Melbourne suburbs, approximately half-way between the Melbourne CBD and Frankston.

How do I book a family portrait session?

Booking is easy, please call (041) 2000 179 to book now, or schedule a complimentary consultation using the discovery call link on this page.

How long will a family portrait session last?

Our family shoots start at 9.30 am and last about 3 hours, including makeup and grooming. After the photo session, you’ll have a 40-minute break for snacks and coffee, with your photo viewing at around 2 pm or earlier. Timing might vary depending on the number of family members.

Is the shoot in the studio or outdoors?

Most of our professional photographic shoots take place in our Melbourne-based studio designed for a five-star experience. Choose a studio photo shoot for modern, classic or candid portraits with clean backgrounds. Or, opt for beach family sessions for natural backgrounds and an adventurous vibe.

What do I get in the end of it all? What are your product options?

We specialize in high-quality finished artwork for your home and bespoke photo albums, covering everything from the shoot and styling to the design of your albums and framed images. We also offer digital copies of all images, depending on your preference. Our clients choose us for our comprehensive services and options that are tailored to each family’s unique needs. To explore all of our product options, please visit the shop.

Can I bring my dog to a family photoshoot?

Please do so by all means! As fellow pet owners, we believe that your furry friend has to appear in your family photos. Let us know in advance, so we can set up the studio accordingly and accommodate your furry friend.

What should we wear?

For a coordinated look, we suggest classic styles, plain colors, or subtle patterns. During our planning session, we’ll help map out your head-to-toe look to ensure that everyone matches and the tones complement your home’s decor.

Do you provide professional hair and makeup service?

Our service includes professional hair and makeup by our talented team. They’ll work with you to achieve the look you want, while enhancing your natural features. You can choose to have your hair and makeup done on the day, or do it yourself. Our team is dedicated to making you feel natural and look your best self for your family photo shoot.

Are you able to remove imperfections from the images?

We can do light touch-ups on skin blemishes, but we won’t alter your appearance. Let us know your concerns before the photo session, and we’ll try our best to address them through posing. If needed, we can also conceal imperfections in post-production.

How many images will we be able to choose from?

On average, we will present you with sixty images to choose from, each of them beautiful on their own and together telling the story of your day. Most clients choose framed prints and an album, which includes all the images in stunning layouts that capture your family’s spirit. We’re confident you’ll love them!

What happens if we are sick?

If you feel unwell, then please let us know as soon as possible and we can postpone your session. We allow one reschedule with no extra charge. At least 24 hours notice is required.

My husband and I don’t like being photographed

We hear you! Most clients tell us that they don’t like to be photographed, but by the end of their session, they’re having so much fun that they don’t want to stop. They always rave about how easy and enjoyable it was, even the husbands do! So, trust us, you’re not alone, but know that having your photos taken doesn’t have to be awkward. You are in capable hands and we know how to make the process smooth for everyone.

What if I don’t like my photos?

While it’s never happened before, we understand that you may have concerns about your satisfaction with the service and product we provide. Please take a look at our customer reviews, where many have shared how much they loved their photos. However, if you’re not completely satisfied, let us know at the design appointment and we’ll arrange a re-shoot at our cost to ensure you love your photos.

Do you photograph newborns and babies?

We do not offer newborn or baby-only sessions. Our family sessions are designed around the whole family together, and children are very welcome as part of that.
