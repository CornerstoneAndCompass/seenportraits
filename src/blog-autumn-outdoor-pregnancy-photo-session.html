Autumn Outdoor Pregnancy Photo Session

By Marina · 2 min read April 23, 2024

You have just nine short months to cherish the little flutters of the first kicks, witness your tummy gently moving, and ponder sweet names for the precious being soon to join the world.

It is a privilege to photograph and craft art that celebrates the remarkable beauty of a feminine body that carries a little life growing within. This time is monumental and must be celebrated in all its splendour!

Creating life is an extraordinary gift: it’s wild, raw, joyful, beautiful, and powerful. Yet, it can also be challenging, filled with a whirlwind of emotions and physical sensations that can feel overwhelming.

As an all-female team (my team and I are mums!), we understand this! Your session is designed with your comfort in mind. We’ll take our time, pamper you, take breaks when needed and ensure you feel stunning in this magical moment. This experience provides an opportunity to connect with the wonder of creating life, to ground yourself in your body and to indulge in some much-needed self-care before your precious bundle of joy arrives.

We take extra care for our mum-to-be clients and provide a safe and supportive environment for your session recognizing that posing can bring extra challenges when you’re nurturing a baby within. A maternity boudoir offers a powerful means to document your journey of creating life, capturing all the remarkable changes along the way.

I’m thrilled to help you craft the artwork that beautifully encapsulates your memories of this special time. These treasures act as a gateway to the past, preserving your family’s legacy and nurturing a sense of belonging for your new baby and generations to come.

Much love, Marina.
