The Grand Opening, Fundraiser and Exhibition

By Marina · 3 min read September 1, 2023

beneath her skin

Exhibition Opening Night & Charity Event in Cheltenham

CELEBRATE FEMINITY WHILE SUPPORTING A GREAT CAUSE

EVENT THAT INSPIRES AND creates CHANGE

We invite you to celebrate our first exhibition at SEEN, enjoy our soul-infused atmosphereand help us raise funds for a great cause supported by Rotary Club.

THE EXHIBITION

Beneath Her Skin is a celebration of a woman in today’s world

She’s had her own journey: a woman, a mother, a wife, a business mogul and a rebel at heart. Caught in daily life, she often feels disconnected from who she is and forgets how joyful and beautiful she can be. She’s torn between conforming to society’s standards and being who she knows she’s meant to be.

At the very core, this exhibition is about the JOY of returning to the truth of who you are as a woman and stepping into your most unapologetic self – the luxury AVAILABLE TO ALL women. No matter your age, size or insecurity in front of the camera, you can be confident, poised, and proud of who you are.

Through the series of emotive photographs portraying the spirit of a modern-day woman, that capture feminine beauty coupled with the power of resounding dignity, we want to invite YOU to embrace the joy of being free in your own skin, give yourself permission to step into your greatness and be seen, most importantly by yourself.

When you delve beneath your skin and embrace all of who you are, you are transforming into your own powerful Seen Queen who steps through life with newfound confidence.

Feel the power of a photograph that captures the embodied beauty of feminine energy in motion, and be empowered by the women that took part in this project that we created together with KunYin Studios.

Let this be your new source of inspiration, grace and impact. The age of invisible women is over. Welcome to SEEN.

THE POWERHOUSE

BEHIND THE INSPIRATION

The Artist

Through her empowering photography Marina helps women to discover their feminine power and reconnect with their wild, fun and spontaneous sides. Marina believes all these aspects are at the core of who the woman is and is something to be incredibly proud of. Her photographic voice speaks to women’s femininity, independence and sexuality through focusing on the raw, authentic, candid expression of yourself. Marina is excited and grateful to be your guide and to capture the natural pure essence of you through photography!

Special Guest and Sponsor

Joanne is the founder of KunYin ~ Feminine Energy in Motion, coach and mentor with over 15 years experience in working with women to live their best lives! A ThetaHealing practitioner and Instructor with experience in understanding the body and mind connection, she believes we can untangle and clear the patterns and beliefs in our body and mind that are holding us back to finally experience the extraordinary, purpose-driven living we desire.

THE CAUSE

A ticket fee and the funds raised at the charity auction will be donated to Rotary Peninsula 2.0 to support women and girls of Ukraine bearing severe impacts of war.

EVENT DETAILS

ADDRESS: 35 Belrose Avenue Cheltenham 3192
