Melbourne Dog Photography Fundraiser for Australian Deaf Dog Rescue

By Marina · 3 min read November 28, 2025

Rosie is a 1.8-year-old Mini Dachshund with a rare double dapple coat – a pattern that gives her those stunning, marbled markings, but is also linked to severe vision impairment.

Rosie was born blind, yet she was kept for breeding because of her colouring. When her puppies were also born blind, the breeders surrendered Rosie and her babies to a shelter and requested they all be euthanised.

Thankfully, Hear No Evil Deaf Dog Rescue stepped in, placed them in loving foster care, and began the search for new homes. I had the honour of donating a portrait session for Rosie and her human, Kat, and today, I’m sharing their story and photos as part of our Pet Glam portrait series celebrating Melbourne’s beloved dogs.

A PET GLAM PORTRAIT EXPERIENCE WITH PURPOSE

Calling all Melbourne dog lovers…

If you’ve ever loved a dog, you already know they aren’t “just pets.” They’re family, they’re joy, they’re the warm heartbeat at our feet. This series was created to celebrate that bond while also helping support animals that need a second chance.

Our Pet Glam Portrait Photography sessions give your dog or cat their own magazine-style photoshoot and yes, it’s as adorable and fun as it sounds! And a portion of every order goes directly to Hear No Evil Deaf Dog Rescue, helping dogs like Rosie receive the care, love, and forever homes they deserve.

Why Rosie’s Story Matters?

Rosie’s journey is a reminder that love sees differently. She may be blind, but she moves through the world with trust, softness, and joy. The way she leans into Kat’s touch… the way her tail wags at the sound of her name… the way she feels love. These portraits honour that bond.

Want to Get Involved?

If you have a dog who means the world to you, this is your chance to celebrate that bond in a way that lasts. Enjoy a Pet Glam portrait experience and take home the artwork you’ll treasure in your home, plus get a feature in our Pet Glam Magazine at the end of the series – a keepsake that highlights the love and personality of your dog. And with every session, you’re also supporting Hear No Evil Deaf Dog Rescue, helping dogs like Rosie find safety and new beginnings.

Celebrate your dog. Create artwork that lasts. Make an impact.
