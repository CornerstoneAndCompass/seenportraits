07

Frequently asked questions

If you have a question that isn’t answered below please give us a call

How far in advance should I book you?

To ensure you receive your branding photos precisely when you need them, we highly recommend booking our professional photography services at least 1 to 2 months in advance. However, if you have a time-sensitive project and need them sooner, please reach out and we’ll do our best to accommodate you in our schedule. Your satisfaction is our top priority, and we’ll work with you to meet your specific needs and deadlines.

Do you provide makeup and hairstyling?

Absolutely! Our incredible team of makeup artists and hairstylists is trained to create enhanced natural camera-ready looks perfect for business photography. We can help groom either women and men, please enquire about it upon booking.

How long will my photo shoot take?

The package you choose will determine the duration of your session. We offer quick 30 minute to one hour headshot sessions, half-day, or full-day personal branding and lifestyle sessions. That being said, we are dedicated to working until we capture the perfect shots and you’re fully satisfied with the results.

What do I need to bring to my photo shoot?

All you need to bring is your gorgeous self, your outfits, and accessories! Typically, we suggest bringing five outfits to your shoot, but the more options you have, the better. We’ll go over all the details beforehand, so you’ll be fully prepared and confident for your shoot day.

Do I bring my own outfits to the shoot?

Yes, you’ll need to bring your own outfits to your photo shoot. We understand that selecting the right attire can be daunting, and that’s why we offer a pre-production strategy meeting where we will help you to choose the style and attire you need for the photo shoot.

How many looks or wardrobe changes do I get?

During your headshot session, you’ll get a guaranteed two different looks, and if you book an all-day personal branding shoot, you’ll have even more options. We don’t set a hard limit on the number of outfit changes you can have. We will do our best to give you as much variety and flexibility as possible.

Do you just take business headshots?

As a personal branding and lifestyle photographer, we can create custom portraits for your blog, socials, website or photos for LinkedIn profile. And, believe it or not, some of my clients have even used their new photos for dating websites! If you require a specific call-to-action image or a unique background with ample space for sales copy, simply ask, and we’ll take care of it all. Your custom images will be crafted to perfection, exactly as you envision them.

Can I have my pictures taken outdoors?

Absolutely! We will discuss the locations for your shoot during the strategy session. Whether you envision your photos being taken on the street, at a local coffee shop, on the beach or in your kitchen, we are committed to giving you the portraits that authentically represent your brand and personality.

How many images do I get?

With the basic headshot package, you’ll receive two high-quality images that are sure to impress. However, often clients love so many of them and it’s difficult to choose just two. When this is the case you can purchase additional ones. All of the photos you receive will be edited in our signature style to ensure they meet our high standards for quality and consistency.

What’s your cancellation policy?

We require a non-refundable deposit in advance upon booking and if you need to reschedule your photo shoot, we are happy to accommodate your request as long as we receive at least 48 hours’ notice.

Can I get a refund if I don’t like my pictures?

You will be seeing your photos along the process as we are taking them, allowing us to make corrections and adjustments to suit your taste, based on your feedback during the shoot. We’ve never been asked for a refund before as we are absolutely committed to giving clients the photos that align with their vision.

How do I pay for my photo session?

We require to pay a deposit to confirm your booking, over the phone or via direct debit. The balance of your final package is due at the time of your viewing and ordering appointment after the photo shoot. We accept payments made via cash, bank transfer, and EFTPOS.

How soon after the shoot do I get my photos?

Your photos will be ready for viewing on the day of your shoot in the studio. Final selection of images will be professionally enhanced and ready for you in two weeks. It might take longer if for some reason you are not available to attend the same day viewing. If you need to rush your images please let us know ahead of time.

Will you travel?

Absolutely! If you’re interested in working together in another city please enquire and we can discuss how we can help.
